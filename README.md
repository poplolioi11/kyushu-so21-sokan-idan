# 桑韓醫談（1713 年刊本，九州大學 ソ-21） / Sōkan idan [Medical Conversations between Japan and Korea]: Transcription Dataset

《桑韓醫談》是正德三年（1713）刊行的漢文醫學筆談及病例集。日本醫者北尾春圃記與朝鮮醫官奇斗文的會晤及問答，討論疾病、飲食與醫療見聞。續有日本醫者的醫論、病例和附錄。可供研究江戶時期的東亞醫學交流、病名與照護經驗。

Sōkan idan is a 1713 Literary Chinese collection of medical conversations and cases. Japanese physician Kitao Shunpo records an encounter and written questions and answers with Korean physician Ki Tu-mun, followed by medical discussions, cases and an appendix. It documents early modern exchange between Japanese and Korean physicians and their accounts of care.

上下卷、附錄、序跋與刊記依九州大學的 47 幅逐欄定位。原有的返點、片假名訓點及可辨夾注另加標記。

The two volumes, appendix, prefaces and colophon are indexed across the holding library’s 47 images. Small annotations, katakana reading marks and return marks are identified separately.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

47幅逐幅回圖盤點，另讀問答、人名、卷界、照護記錄及刊記，依原圖採用 6 項訂正。覆核為逐幅選讀。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。夾注保留圖幅及部分相對位置。訓點逐幅辨層，部分可讀字另記，尚未逐點轉錄。

All 47 images received a separate inventory and selected readings of dialogue, names, volume boundaries, care records and the colophon. 6 image-supported changes are documented. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Interlinear notes retain scan references and selected positions. Reading marks are identified on each relevant image, with selected legible marks transcribed.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
